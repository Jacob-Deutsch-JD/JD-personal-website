Rovewild — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Rovewild (“we”, “us”) is an outdoor mapping app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It applies to the Rovewild web app and the Rovewild Android and iOS apps.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we store your email address and an encrypted password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase). You may also set a display username and an optional profile photo.
  • Your content. Map pins, drawn routes/paths, folders, notes, and their styling that you create in the app. These are stored so they sync across your devices when you are signed in.
  • App settings. Your preferences (units, language, basemap, layer toggles, marker categories, etc.) so they can sync across your devices when you enable settings sync.
  • Location. If you tap the “locate me” control (or grant location permission), the app reads your device’s current location on the device to center the map. We do not track your location in the background and we do not store your location history on our servers.
  • Basic technical data. Standard request/diagnostic information (e.g. app version, error logs) needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How we use it

  • To provide the core features: showing the map, saving and syncing your pins, routes, folders, notes, and settings, and signing you in.
  • To keep the service secure and working (diagnostics, abuse prevention).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or ad tracking.

How your data is stored and shared

  • Your account and content are stored in our database (Supabase, which hosts Postgres/Auth/Storage). Access is restricted so that a signed-in user can only read and write their own data (enforced by row-level security).
  • Map tiles and reference data (basemap, terrain, points of interest, and optional overlays) are served as static files from our content delivery network (Cloudflare / Supabase Storage).
  • Some optional map features fetch public, third-party data directly from your device (for example Wikipedia and other public map data sources) when you use them. Those requests are subject to those services’ own privacy policies.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We share personal data only with the service providers above that are necessary to run the app, or where required by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Access / export / deletion. You can request a copy or deletion of your account and associated content by contacting us at jacobadeutschwork@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your pins, routes, folders, notes, and synced settings from our servers.
  • Location permission. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the map still works, it just won’t center on you.
  • Local-only use. Content and settings you create while signed out stay on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Data retention

We keep your account and content until you delete them or ask us to. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed to operate the service.

Children

Rovewild is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we’ll change the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app or by email.
