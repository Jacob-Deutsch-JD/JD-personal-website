Astronomy display options
Astronomy calculations use Astronomy Engine. Normal meteor-shower peaks use the IMO Working List (June 2025) and are calculated for the selected year from each shower's J2000 solar longitude, so they are not limited to 2026 or 2027. Year-specific predicted meteor outbursts are not included. The location choices are broad regions represented by a central point, so local eclipse visibility is approximate near the edge of an eclipse path.
