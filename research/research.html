Research

In the summer of 2025 I researched and wrote a sole authorship paper on "Finding Inter-species Associations on Large Citizen Science Datasets". Essentially I wanted to see if ecological relationships could be revealed within iNaturalist data. It turns out that they can be! I've submitted my research for publication and have a preprint out >>here<<. My Arxiv.org abstract is found >>here<< as well. I presented this research at the Bay Area Wildlife Symposium on November 8, 2025.
