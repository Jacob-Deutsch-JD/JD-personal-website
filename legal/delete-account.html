Delete your Rovewild account and data

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Rovewild account and the data associated with it. It applies to the Rovewild app (net.rovewild.app) and the Rovewild web app.

How to request deletion

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the email address associated with your Rovewild account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify the request against your account and process it.

  1. Send the email from your account’s email address.
  2. Include the subject line Delete my account.
  3. We confirm the request and delete your account and associated data (see below).

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile (username and avatar)
  • Your content: map pins, drawn routes/paths, folders, notes, and their styling
  • Your synced app settings

Deleting only some of your data

If you want to remove specific content (for example, certain pins or routes) without deleting your whole account, you can either delete those items directly in the app, or email CONTACT_EMAIL describing what you would like removed.

Retention

Account-deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 90 days. We may retain a minimal record where required by law.
